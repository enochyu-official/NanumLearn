We are here!

Author: Enoch Yu | August 16, 2026 | 2 min read | 216 words

Hi there! As the initial post, I hope to provide a glimpse on what we have been doing so far and few improvements that will be made as we progress.

After the initialization and formalizing our purpose and goals, I have been making and designing this website. There are improvements to be made in the website, but we hope to get them as we progress. With the empty website, I have been adding the resources that I have created and that I am working on right now as the initial start. We also have been sharing our ideas and the purpose of Nanum Learn with others to receive feedback and improve. These are the works that have been done so far before the initial release.

That being said, we have multiple areas left for improvements. Such areas include, but are not limited to:

  • Gathering more members to accelerate our development
  • Sharing this platform
  • Creating more resources outside competition math and higher level math
  • Planning upcoming classes
  • Web design (I tried my best to make it look good overall in most of the mobile devices, but we still need help!)

This is it for our current status! Thank you for your interest, and please don’t forget to join us for more updates and notice on future classes!
